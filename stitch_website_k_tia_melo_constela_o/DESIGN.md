## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-end editorial and therapeutic experience tailored for Kátia Melo, balancing systemic healing authority with deep human warmth. The aesthetic roots itself in contemporary editorial design: generous margins, tactile linen-like warm tones, fine hairline golden accents, and intentional typographic hierarchies. 

Targeted toward individuals seeking emotional clarity, resolution of ancestral/family ties, and self-knowledge, the visual language intentionally repudiates cliché esoteric tropes, over-saturated spiritual glows, or clinical coldness. Instead, it projects the grounding presence of an attorney, author, and systemic facilitator. The emotional response is one of safety, reverence, inner peace, and decisive readiness to take action via personal consultation.

## Colors

The color palette draws from organic linen textures, warm human skin, botanical greenery, and deep contemplative waters:

- **Primary (`#173F6B`)**: Deep Navy. Serves as the bedrock of intellectual authority, structural contrast, primary headlines, and anchoring interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#C98273`)**: Soft Terracotta / Rosé. Evokes empathy, tenderness, emotional release, and ancestral ties. Used for highlighted phrases, badges, and warm focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#C8A97E`)**: Champagne Gold. Adds refined polish and subtle luminosity to dividers, icon strokes, borders, and gentle accents without harsh glare.
- **Neutral (`#F8F4EE`)**: Raw Linen Off-White. The baseline canvas tone, replacing stark sterile white with an organic, breathable, calm atmosphere. Supporting tones include `#F2EBE1` for card surfaces and `#2D2926` for high-legibility body copy.
- **Accent Olive (`#8B8E68`)**: Soft Botanical Olive. Denotes organic growth, stability, roots, and gentle reconciliation; ideal for testimonial backdrops and secondary CTAs.
- **WhatsApp Conversion (`#25D366` / `#1E7E34`)**: Dedicated conversion tokens retained for instant high-visibility contact buttons, integrated cleanly into golden and warm backgrounds.

## Typography

Typography acts as the voice of the facilitation: poised, human, and grounded.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements**: Set in **Playfair Display**. Offers classical grace, high contrast, and refined weight without being stiff.
- **Accents & Emotional Highlights**: Styled with italicized serif variants (`script-highlight` / Playfair Display Italic) for lead words such as *"escondidas nas histórias"*, *"raízes"*, and *"passado"*, pairing seamlessly with delicate calligraphic touches.
- **Body & Conversion UI**: Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Ensures razor-sharp legibility on small viewports, approachable friendliness, and clean contrast against the serif headlines.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows**: Rendered in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with generous letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to punctuate structural categories like "CONSTELAÇÃO FAMILIAR SISTÊMICA".

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the breathing room found in curated art and high-end editorial publications:

- **Desktop & Wide Displays**: A 12-column layout centered with generous margins (`margin: 3rem`) and broad section gutters (`space-xl: 3.5rem`), prioritizing asymmetrical arrangements where portraits and textual quotes balance naturally.
- **Mobile Viewports**: Realigns into a single fluid column with `1.25rem` screen padding to ensure full thumb reach and eliminate micro-cramping.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Substantial inter-section air creates pauses for reflection, which is crucial for sensitive therapeutic subjects. Elements are grouped using organic offsets rather than dense technical grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile linen layers, warm diffused drop surfaces, and metallic golden hairlines:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base background uses `#F8F4EE`. Elevating elements (cards, booking panels, testimonials) transition to `#FFFFFF` or `#F2EBE1`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Structural borders are thin (`1px`) and tinted with champagne gold (`rgba(200, 169, 126, 0.4)`) or terracotta tints, maintaining delicate structure without visual weight.
- **Ambient Shadowing**: Drop shadows are soft, warm-tinted, and diffused: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 63, 107, 0.06)`, steering clear of harsh synthetic gray shadows.
- **Organic Cutouts & Arc Framing**: Curved overlays and circular botanical frames elevate photographic portraits into organic, human shapes.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, welcoming corners (`0.5rem` baseline, expanding to `1rem` - `1.5rem` for content cards) combined with circular motifs reflecting family circles, wooden constellation figurines, and systemic order:

- **Interactive Nodes & Badges**: Fully pill-rounded (`9999px`) for quick tactile affordance and friendliness.
- **Card & Media Frames**: Soft `12px` to `20px` borders, occasionally stylized with asymmetric arch cutouts (e.g., top-left / top-right arched at `48px` to evoke classical architectural doorways and systemic portals).
- **Subtle Circular Line Motifs**: Thin golden circular vector rings around focal images evoke Bert Hellinger's orders of systemic balance.

## Components

### Primary Conversion CTA (WhatsApp & Direct Booking)
- **Visuals**: Full pill-shaped button with rich deep navy (`#173F6B`) or energetic WhatsApp forest green (`#1E7E34`), accented with subtle gold outline or drop shadow.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Bold, `16px`, centered with an icon prefix (WhatsApp chat bubble or calendar badge).
- **States**: Hover subtly lifts (`translateY(-2px)`) with an expanded warm shadow (`0 8px 20px rgba(23, 63, 107, 0.18)`).

### Secondary & Outlined Actions
- **Visuals**: Transparent background, `1.5px` border in champagne gold (`#C8A97E`) or deep navy (`#173F6B`), pill-shaped.
- **Interaction**: Subtle fill on hover with light terracotta tint (`rgba(201, 130, 115, 0.08)`).

### Editorial Quote & Testimonial Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#F2EBE1` or crisp white, accented with an oversized decorative quote mark in deep navy (`#173F6B`, `48px`) or soft olive (`#8B8E68`).
- **Typography**: Playfair Display for key insights, followed by Plus Jakarta Sans (`14px`) for participant identification and systemic themes.
- **Borders**: Thin warm champagne borders with an optional soft olive bottom wave.

### Category Chips & Systemic Pillars
- **Visuals**: Pill container with linen background (`#EDE5D8`) and terracotta (`#C98273`) or navy text.
- **Usage**: Used for session tags ("Online", "Presencial", "Constelação Individual", "Bonecos").

### Form Fields & Intake Inputs
- **Base**: Cream background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px` solid `#D9CFC4`.
- **Focus**: Border shifts to deep navy `#173F6B` with a gentle 3px outer glow in `#C8A97E26`. Clean sans-serif typography ensures effortless client data entry.
